Run this once, before anything else, to create seat documents 01–45 in Firestore (01–06 locked as reserved). Safe to click again later — it only resets seats that are still available, never a seat someone already holds.

2. Draw the ballot

Randomly assigns a ballot number to every officer on the roster who doesn't have one yet, then opens the board for picking.

3. Priority quota / direct assignment

For SVPs or VPs who opt for immediate priority, or to assign on behalf of an officer who is away — this bypasses the queue.
